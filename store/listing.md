**Title** (26)

```
Warfare 1347: Medieval War
```

**Short description** (76)

```
Medieval war sandbox: lead armies, storm castles and claim the crown of 1347
```

**Full description**
```
The year is 1347. Plague is coming from the east, and four kingdoms fight over Eurasia. Start as a nameless vassal with a handful of peasants — and end as a king.

Warfare 1347 is a medieval sandbox in the spirit of Mount & Blade, built for your phone: a living world map, armies, sieges and tournaments. Single-player, fully offline.

⚔ FIELD BATTLES
• Side-view battles with up to 36 fighters per side and reinforcements in waves
• Deploy before the fight, pick formations (line, shield wall, wedge, hedgehog) and give orders to each group
• Couched-lance charges, flank and rear attacks, morale and rout — chase the fleeing enemy off the field
• Weather and terrain matter: rain soaks bowstrings, hills give archers extra range
• Lead your hero in person or let the auto-battle decide

🏰 SIEGES AND CONQUEST
• Storm castles and cities: archers on the walls, gates and ladders
• Lords of every kingdom march on campaigns, besiege fortresses and make peace on their own
• Earn a fief from your liege, build walls, a market and barracks — or found your own kingdom

🛡 HERO AND WARBAND
• 99 items: from gambeson to Gothic plate, swords, axes, bows and horses — your hero’s look changes with the gear
• 70 kinds of troops: a peasant can grow into a knight, a berserker or a horse archer
• Each kingdom has special troops: hand-gunners, berserkers, lasso riders, naffatun with Greek fire
• 10 companions with their own temper: they command your infantry, archers and cavalry and let you lead a bigger army
• Troop wages, prisoners and ransom, mercenaries in taverns

🌍 A LIVING WORLD OF 1347
• A map of Eurasia with real coastlines: 34 towns, 18 castles and 83 villages
• The Black Death spreads along the trade routes
• Caravans, bandits, deserters and road events
• Tournaments: knockout duels and team mêlées, bets and prize gear
• Quests from lords and village elders, relations with the nobility, marriage and your own coat of arms

📱 MADE FOR MOBILE
• No internet or sign-up required
• Time on the map only moves while your party moves — perfect for short sessions
• A short prologue that teaches you as you play
• Three save slots, autosave with a backup copy
• English, Русский, Português, Türkçe

Raise an army, win glory and take the crown. All of Eurasia awaits.
```
